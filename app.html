Illustrative planning arithmetic only. Every rate is a labeled default, and the bands below name capacity, not permission to fly. Where your device prints a watt-hour rating, use that number instead of any estimate. Airline rules live with the airline: many carriers also cap how many power banks you may carry and do not allow recharging one onboard, so check your carrier's current rules before the flight, and never pack a lithium bank in checked luggage.
